Check in on the people you love, even when you can't be with them

A quick tap to say "I'm OK", once a day or up to three times. If a check-in is missed, your family finds out.

For the times in between

Nothing replaces visiting, a phone call or a hug, and Daily OK isn't meant to. It's for the days in between, when you can't be there.

Messaging apps are great for staying in touch, but they don't remind anyone to check in, and they can't tell you when someone has gone quiet. Daily OK does both: it gently reminds your loved one, and it lets you know if a check-in is missed.
