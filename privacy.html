Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Daily OK is a check-in app that lets a person (the "person checking in") tell their family each day that they're OK, and alerts the family if a check-in is missed. This policy explains what information Daily OK uses, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Daily OK is provided by Tom Bettle, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: dailyok.help@icloud.com.

We do not collect your email address, a password, contacts, location, photos, or an advertising identifier. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

When the person checking in links their phone, the app shows exactly what their family will see and asks them to agree first. We record when they agreed.

Our legal basis

We use your information to provide the service you've asked for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and in our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working (Article 6(1)(f)).

Mood can be information about health. We only record it if the person checking in chooses a mood, which is their explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). Choosing a mood is always optional: you can tap "Skip", and you can withdraw consent at any time by not choosing a mood or by deleting your data.

Who can see it

Only the members of your own family group in Daily OK: the person checking in, and the family members they've linked with using a code. We never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

We use two service providers to run the app, who process information only on our instructions:

  • Supabase, which hosts the database in the European Union (Ireland). Transfers from the UK to the EU are covered by the UK's adequacy regulations.
  • Apple Push Notification service, which delivers notifications to your iPhone.
  • Apple's App Store, which handles Daily OK Plus subscriptions and payments.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your family uses Daily OK. You can delete it at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my data (family members) or Settings → Unlink this phone and delete my data (the person checking in). This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your profile and your check-ins. When the last family member deletes their data, the whole family group and all its history are deleted. If a phone is unlinked or never joins a family, its account is deleted automatically after 30 days. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

You have the right to access the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to receive a copy of it. To use any of these rights, email dailyok.help@icloud.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

All information is sent over encrypted connections and stored in an encrypted database. Security rules make sure each family can only ever see its own information.

Children

Daily OK is not intended for children under 13.

Not a medical or emergency service

Daily OK is a wellbeing check-in tool. It is not a medical device, and it doesn't replace emergency services, personal alarms or professional care. Notifications can be delayed or not delivered, for example if a phone is off, out of signal, or has notifications switched off. In an emergency, call 999.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
