Common questions

How do I link my parent's phone?

On your phone, choose "I'm looking out for someone", then "Set up Daily OK", and tap "Show code". On their phone, choose "I'm checking in each day" and enter the 6-digit code.

How do I add a brother, sister or neighbour?

Go to Settings → Invite a family member. On their phone, they choose "I'm looking out for someone", then "Join my family", and enter the code.

I'm not getting alerts

Check that notifications are on for Daily OK in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Daily OK, and that Time Sensitive Notifications is switched on so alerts get through Focus and Do Not Disturb. Daily OK shows a warning at the top of the screen if notifications are off.

My parent has a new phone

It takes about 2 minutes, and it's easiest when you're with them or on a video call.

Before you start: the new iPhone is set up, and Daily OK is installed from the App Store. If everything was moved across from the old phone (Quick Start or an iCloud backup), open Daily OK first: if it already shows their "Good morning…" screen with the I'm OK button, you're done.

On your phone (any family member can do this):

  1. Open Daily OK and tap Settings.
  2. In the Family section, tap Link a new phone.
  3. A 6-digit code appears. It works for 24 hours and can be used once.

On their new phone:

  1. Open Daily OK and tap I'm checking in each day.
  2. Type their first name and the 6-digit code.
  3. Read "What your family will see" together, then switch on I'm happy to share this with my family.
  4. Tap Link this phone.
  5. When asked "Allow notifications?", tap Allow, so their reminders appear.

What happens automatically: their check-in history, check-in times and settings are kept, reminders are set up on the new phone, and the old phone is unlinked so it can no longer check in for them.

Quick check: ask them to tap I'm OK on the new phone, and you should see it straight away. If a red "Reminders are off" card appears on their screen, tap Open Settings → Notifications → switch on Allow Notifications.

I have a new phone (family member)

Ask another family member, or the person checking in, to go to Settings → Invite a family member. On your new phone, choose "I'm looking out for someone", then "Join my family", and enter the code. Everything you could see before is still there.

We're going on holiday

Use away mode: Settings → Away. No reminders or missed check-in alerts until the date you choose. Everyone in the family is told when it starts and ends.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete my data. See the privacy policy for exactly what is deleted.
